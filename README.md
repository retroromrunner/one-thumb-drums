# Dream in Music

A lucid drumming dream — Ultimate AI-Powered Game Jam #7 entry.
Themes: **DREAM** (mandatory) + **STAGE** (hidden Theme 2, revealed at jam start).

You're dreaming — and you're on stage. The band has no drummer: **you** are
the dream's drummer, in the spotlight. Dream fragments drift down like clouds — tap the matching pad (Kick, Snare,
Hi-Hat) just as a cloud crosses the gold line. Stardust is optional free play.
Keep the beat, don't wake up.

## The four dreams

1. **Dream I · Cathedral Rain** — Easy · 100 BPM · dark/goth
2. **Dream II · Iron Pulse** — Medium · 120 BPM · driving rock
3. **Dream III · Night Shift** — Hard · 140 BPM · fast Phrygian metal
4. **Dream IV · Ironclad** — Expert · 156 BPM · heavy metal, clean intro

## How it was built

Assembled in the jam window (Nov 13–16, 2026) from prepared assets: the
One-Thumb drum engine, real CC0 drum samples, and the four original CC0
backing songs. The game itself — dream framing, cloud notes, rainbow-swirl
night sky, twinkling stars, new title and copy — was built fresh in-window.
It is a distinct new game, not the pre-existing One-Thumb Drums.

Scoring: Perfect ±70 ms = 300 pts · Good ±140 ms = 100 pts · a miss stirs
you awake and breaks your combo. One thumb is all you need; play on your phone.

AI disclosure: built with AI assistance (code, visuals, audio mixing).
